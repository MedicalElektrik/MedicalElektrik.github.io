Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Who we are

ME Publisher is a private tool of CV. Medical Elektrik, Indonesia. It publishes the company's own approved content to the company's own social media accounts. This policy explains what it keeps and why.

2. What we keep

  • For each connected account: the account's id and name, and the sign-in (access and refresh tokens) that the platform gave us when the company connected it.
  • For each post: the video or photo, caption and hashtags that the team prepared. Files are copied to a temporary working folder only to publish, and are deleted within a few hours.
  • During sign-in: one short-lived cookie (valid for 10 minutes) that makes sure the sign-in is finished in the same browser that started it.

3. What we do not collect

We do not read or store messages, comments from other people, followers, viewers, or any other person's data. We use no advertising, no tracking cookies and no analytics.

4. How we use it

Only to publish the team's approved content to the connected accounts, to read back the link of each post, and to keep the sign-in working (for example, renewing a token). Nothing is sold or shared. Information goes only to the platform the team chose to post to (TikTok, Meta, Google/YouTube), which is what publishing means.

5. Where it is kept

Sign-ins are kept privately in the company's own Google Apps Script project, not in the pages of the app, and are never shown to the team's pages or to other users.

6. What each platform gives us

  • TikTok: your account's basic profile (open id, display name, avatar) and permission to upload videos to the inbox as drafts. Nothing is posted publicly by the Service.
  • Instagram and Threads: permission to read the account's basic profile and to publish posts (and, where the team asks for it, a first comment on its own post).
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the connected channel and to read the channel's basic details. The Service uses YouTube API Services; by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

7. Removing a connection and deleting data

  • Remove the account in the ME Team app (Marketing, Accounts). Its sign-in is then marked unusable and is no longer used.
  • Or withdraw the permission in the platform itself: TikTok (Settings, Security and permissions, Apps), Instagram and Threads (Settings, Apps and websites), Google (Security, Third-party access).
  • When a platform tells us a connection was removed (for example Meta's deauthorize and data deletion requests), we mark that sign-in as unusable and no longer use it. To ask us to delete anything else, contact us below.

8. Children

The Service is a business tool and is not directed at children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
