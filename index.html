ME Publisher

Internal publishing tool of CV. Medical Elektrik, Indonesia

ME Publisher is the tool Medical Elektrik's own marketing team uses to publish the company's approved content (videos, photos and captions) to the company's own social media accounts, at the times the team sets in its internal ME Team app.

What it does

  • Posts to the company's own accounts on Instagram, Threads and YouTube, and sends videos to the company's own TikTok inbox as drafts, which a team member then reviews and posts in the TikTok app.
  • Works only for accounts that the company has connected itself, through each platform's own sign-in and consent screen.
  • Reads nothing about other people: no followers, messages or viewer data.

Who it is for

It is a private tool for the Medical Elektrik team. It is not a public service, it has no public sign-up, and it is not offered to other businesses or individuals.
